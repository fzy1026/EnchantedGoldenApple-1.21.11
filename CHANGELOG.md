# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-17

Initial release.

### Added
- Golden Apples are now enchantable with any enchantment.
- Eating an enchanted Golden Apple grants potion effects based on its enchantments (43 mappings).
- 22 new mob effects with custom icons and bilingual (English / 简体中文) names.

### Notes
- Effect amplifier = ⌊enchantment level ÷ 2⌋, duration = enchantment level × 10 seconds.
